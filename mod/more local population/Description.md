# More Local Population

Added 2 building types, one aimed to increase local population and another to decrease. Check README for update log.

**This is a cheating mod. The purpose is to remove development restrictions of the game in the later phase.**

Considering small AI countries in large market have high tendency to build these building, which may bring tremendous pressure to the game, Tech limit is added and the output of basic production methods is reduced.

The population production center aims to increase local population. The production method will change its effect:

* Base: 10% birth rate, 1 unit of  rubber and oil for 10 unit of tools.
* Advanced(Unlocking: Electrical Generation) : 15% birth rate, 60 unit of rubber and oil for 10 unit of tools.
* Super(Unlocking: Electrical Railway): 20% of birth rate, 100 unit rubber and oil, plus 30 unit of iron for 10 unit of tools.

The meat production center aims to decrease local population. The effects are:

* Base: 3% mortality multiplication, 10 unit of coal, meat, oil and fish for 10 unit of tools.
* Advanced(Unlocking: Dynamite): 5% mortality multiplication, 30 unit of coal, fish and meat, 60 unit of oil and iron for 10 unit of tools.
* Super(Unlocking: Aniline): 6% of mortality multiplication, 60 unit of coal and iron, 50 unit of meat and fish, and 100 unit of oil for 10 unit of tools.
* Tremendous(Unlocking: Electrical Generation): 20% of mortality multiplication, 60 unit of coal and iron, 80 unit of meat and fish, 120 unit of oil.
